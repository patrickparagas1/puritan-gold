PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Sealed with the Holy Spirit of Promise"
July 4, 2026

---

"In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will: that we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted in Christ. In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory." -- Ephesians 1:11-14

The apostle now brings before us three glorious realities that belong to every true believer: an inheritance, a seal, and an earnest. Each one is a cause for unbounded joy and unshakeable confidence.

First, we have obtained an inheritance. The word in the original language may also be translated 'we were made God's inheritance.' This is a staggering thought -- not only do we inherit God's blessings, but God counts us as His inheritance, His treasured possession. As the Puritan Thomas Manton wrote, 'God's people are His portion, His jewels, His peculiar treasure. He values them above all the works of His hands.'

Second, we were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise. In the ancient world, a seal served three purposes: it was a mark of ownership, a guarantee of authenticity, and a pledge of security. When God seals us with His Spirit, He marks us as His own, He authenticates our faith as genuine, and He guarantees that we shall be kept unto the day of final redemption. No forger can break this seal. No thief can steal what God has sealed. No power in the universe can unseal what the Almighty has closed.

Third, the Spirit is the earnest -- the down payment, the first installment -- of our inheritance. When you taste the joy of the Spirit in worship, that is a foretaste of heaven. When you feel the peace of God that passes understanding, that is an advance payment on the eternal rest. When you experience the love of God shed abroad in your heart, that is a sample of the ocean of love in which you shall swim forever.

Richard Sibbes, the heavenly doctor, used to say that the comforts of the Spirit in this life are but the bunch of grapes brought from Canaan -- enough to show us what the land is like, enough to make us long for the fullness that awaits us, but nothing compared to the abundance that shall be ours when we cross the Jordan and enter the promised land.

And once again, the grand purpose rings out: 'unto the praise of his glory.' This is the refrain of the whole chapter, the theme song of redemption. We were chosen for His glory. We were redeemed for His glory. We were sealed for His glory. And we shall be glorified -- for His glory. Amen.
