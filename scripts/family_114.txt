PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 14
"The Way That Seems Right"
Proverbs 14 -- Proverbs 14 -- Family Devotional

---

Good evening, beloved family. Let us draw near to God together.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 14. Let me read our key passage:

"There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death."
That is Proverbs 14:12.

There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death. Proverbs 14, verse 12, is one of the most sobering verses in all of Scripture. Some paths look perfectly fine on the surface. They seem reasonable, safe, even wise. But they lead somewhere terrible.

Jonathan Edwards warned his listeners about the deceitfulness of the human heart. We are very good at convincing ourselves that what we want to do is the right thing to do. We can come up with reasons and excuses for almost anything. That is why we cannot trust our own feelings alone. We need a higher standard. We need God's Word.

How do we avoid the way that seems right but leads to destruction? By testing every decision, every plan, every desire against the truth of Scripture. Does this choice honor God? Does it line up with what the Bible teaches? Would I be comfortable making this choice in front of Jesus?

These are hard questions, but they are life-saving questions. Let us be a family that asks them honestly and follows God's way, even when it is not the popular way or the easy way.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death. -- Proverbs 14:12"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: Why do some wrong choices look right at first?

Number 2: How can we check our decisions against God's Word?

Number 3: Can you think of a time the easy choice was not the right choice?

Let us pray together. Lord God, we thank You for this time as a family. Thank You for speaking to us through Proverbs chapter 14. Plant these truths deep in our hearts, especially in the hearts of our children. Help us to walk in wisdom tomorrow and every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Rest well tonight. God is faithful, and His wisdom is sure.
