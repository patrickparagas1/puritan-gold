PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Be Filled with the Spirit"
July 22, 2026

---

"And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit; speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord." -- Ephesians 5:15-21

'Be filled with the Spirit.' This is not a suggestion but a command, and it is in the present tense, meaning we are to be continually, habitually filled. Just as a drunkard is controlled by wine, so the believer is to be controlled by the Spirit. But while wine leads to dissipation and ruin, the Spirit leads to worship, gratitude, and mutual submission. John Owen wrote three massive volumes on the work of the Holy Spirit, and his conclusion was this: the Spirit-filled life is not marked primarily by ecstatic experiences or miraculous gifts, but by the steady production of Christ-like character -- love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. The evidence of being filled with the Spirit, Paul tells us, is corporate worship: speaking to one another in psalms and hymns, singing and making melody in the heart. A Spirit-filled church is a singing church, a thankful church, a submissive church. And the fountain from which all these virtues flow is a heart that is continually being renewed by the presence and power of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
