PURITAN GOLD -- The Glory of Christ: Complete Summary A Comprehensive Overview of John Owen's Classic Meditation The Glory of Christ. By John Owen. A Complete Summary. Welcome to this summary of John Owen's The Glory of Christ, one of the last and most devotional works of the greatest English Puritan theologian. Written near the end of Owen's life, this book is a sustained meditation on the glory of Christ as revealed in Scripture and experienced by faith. Owen's central thesis is that the beholding of Christ's glory is the most transforming and sanctifying exercise available to the Christian soul. He who beholds Christ by faith is changed into His image from glory to glory. In Part One, Owen explores the glory of Christ from multiple angles. He begins with Christ as the only representative of God to the Church. He then examines the glory of the mysterious constitution of Christ's person, the union of two natures in one person. Owen traces the glory of Christ in His condescension, His love, and the discharge of His mediatory office. He shows the glory of Christ in His exaltation, in Old Testament representations, in His intimate conjunction with the Church, and in the communication of Himself unto believers. Owen explores the recapitulation of all things in Christ and then compares our present beholding of Christ's glory by faith with the future vision of His glory in heaven. In Part Two, Owen turns to practical application. He calls believers to recover from spiritual decay by fresh contemplation of Christ's glory. The great remedy for coldness of heart and weakness of faith is to return to beholding the beauty and excellency of Jesus Christ. This work remains one of the supreme treasures of Christian devotional literature.
