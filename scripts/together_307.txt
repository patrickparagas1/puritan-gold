PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 7
"Holding Fast to Wisdom"
Proverbs 7 -- Day 7 -- Couples Devotional

---

Welcome, husband and wife. There is something powerful about two hearts turning toward God at the same time. Let us do that now, together.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 7. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"My son, keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee. Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye."
That is Proverbs 7:1-2.

Solomon urges us to keep God's commandments close, like the apple of our eye. For a married couple, this means making God's Word the standard by which you measure everything in your marriage.

Thomas Watson taught: Thomas Watson taught that the Word of God, hidden in the heart, is the believer's strongest shield against temptation. He that fills his mind with Scripture leaves no room for the enemy's suggestions.

When couples saturate their minds with Scripture, they build a defense against the temptations and deceptions that threaten every marriage. The world offers many counterfeit versions of love, intimacy, and fulfillment. But God's Word reveals the real thing. Hold fast to it together.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

How can we protect our marriage from the subtle temptations of the world? What safeguards should we put in place?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for protection over your marriage. Ask God to surround you both with wisdom and to shield you from the enemy's schemes.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

The Lord bless you and keep you both. May He make His face shine upon your marriage. Goodnight.
