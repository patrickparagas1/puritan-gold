PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 13
"Walking with the Wise"
Proverbs 13 -- Proverbs 13 -- Family Devotional

---

Welcome, family. It is so good to be together in God's Word tonight.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 13. Let me read our key passage:

"He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed."
That is Proverbs 13:20.

He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed. Proverbs chapter 13, verse 20, is one of the most practical verses in the whole Bible. Your friends shape who you become.

Richard Baxter wrote that the people you spend the most time with will have more influence on your character than almost anything else. If you walk with those who fear the Lord, you will grow in the fear of the Lord. But if you walk with those who mock God and ignore His ways, their attitudes will slowly rub off on you, even if you do not realize it.

This does not mean we should be unkind to people who do not know God. Not at all! But it does mean we should be intentional about who our closest friends and companions are. The people we trust, confide in, and spend the most time with should be people who love the Lord and encourage us to grow.

As a family, let us pray for wise friends. Let us ask God to bring people into our lives who will sharpen us and encourage our faith. And let us be that kind of friend to others too.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed. -- Proverbs 13:20"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: Who are the wise people in your life?

Number 2: How do your friends influence who you are becoming?

Number 3: What makes someone a wise friend to walk with?

Let us close in prayer. Heavenly Father, thank You for gathering our family together in Your Word tonight. Thank You for the wisdom of Proverbs chapter 13. Help us to live out what we have learned. Guard our hearts, guide our steps, and fill our home with Your love and peace. We pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.

Until tomorrow, family. May the Lord bless you and keep you.
