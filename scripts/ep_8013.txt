PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"The Unsearchable Riches of Christ"
July 13, 2026

---

"Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, is this grace given, that I should preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ." -- Ephesians 3:8-13

'Unsearchable riches' -- what a phrase! The Greek word means 'untraceable,' like a path through a forest so vast that no one can map its boundaries. The riches of Christ are inexhaustible. You may spend an eternity exploring them and never reach the end. Thomas Goodwin said that heaven itself will be an eternal unfolding of the riches of Christ, and that after ten thousand years of discovery, we shall feel as though we have only just begun. Paul counts himself 'less than the least of all saints' -- a remarkable expression that shows how deeply the memory of his former persecution of the church had humbled him. True greatness in the kingdom of God is always accompanied by deep humility. The man who has seen most of God's glory is the man who is most conscious of his own unworthiness. Spurgeon once said, 'The more a man grows in grace, the less he thinks of himself.' This is not false modesty or self-deprecation; it is the natural response of a soul that has been overwhelmed by the grace of God. When you stand at the foot of a mountain, you feel your own smallness. When you stand at the foot of Calvary, you feel it infinitely more. Amen.
