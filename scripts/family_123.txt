PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 23
"Giving God Your Heart"
Proverbs 23 -- Proverbs 23 -- Family Devotional

---

Welcome, family. It is so good to be together in God's Word tonight.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 23. Let me read our key passage:

"My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways."
That is Proverbs 23:26.

My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways. Proverbs 23, verse 26. Of all the things God could ask for, He asks for the one thing that is most precious: your heart.

God does not just want our good behavior. He does not just want our church attendance. He does not just want us to follow rules. He wants our hearts. He wants our love, our affection, our devotion, our trust. He wants all of us.

Thomas Brooks wrote that giving God your heart is not a one-time event. It is a daily practice. Every morning we wake up and say, Lord, here is my heart again. Today, it is Yours. Direct it, fill it, use it for Your glory. And every evening, we examine whether we truly gave Him our hearts that day, or whether we kept parts of it for ourselves.

As a family, let us give God our hearts tonight. Not just the easy parts, not just the parts we are proud of, but all of it. The fears, the doubts, the struggles, everything. He can handle it all, and He will fill us with more joy and peace than we ever thought possible.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways. -- Proverbs 23:26"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: What does it mean to give God your heart?

Number 2: Is there a part of your life you have not given to God yet?

Number 3: How can we give our hearts to God more fully as a family?

Let us pray together. Lord God, we thank You for this time as a family. Thank You for speaking to us through Proverbs chapter 23. Plant these truths deep in our hearts, especially in the hearts of our children. Help us to walk in wisdom tomorrow and every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Until tomorrow, family. May the Lord bless you and keep you.
