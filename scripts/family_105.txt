PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 5
"Walking the Right Path"
Proverbs 5 -- Proverbs 5 -- Family Devotional

---

Hello and welcome to our family devotional. Come, let us sit together and listen to what God has to say.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 5. Let me read our key passage:

"For the ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD, and he pondereth all his goings."
That is Proverbs 5:21.

Proverbs chapter 5 has a message that is important for every age: be careful which path you walk on. In life, there are many roads we can take. Some look exciting and fun, but they lead to places we do not want to go. Other paths seem harder at first, but they lead to safety, joy, and peace.

John Owen, who wrote much about the Christian's daily battle against sin, taught that sin never announces itself honestly. It never says, Hello, I am here to ruin your life. Instead, it dresses up in attractive clothing and whispers, This will be fun. Come, try it just once. That is why we must learn to recognize the wrong path before we start walking down it.

How do we do that? By knowing God's Word so well that we can spot a lie when we hear one. The Bible is like a lamp for our feet, as the Psalmist wrote. It lights up the path so we can see where we are going. Without it, we stumble in the dark.

As a family, let us commit to walking the right path together. When one of us starts to stray, the others can gently say, Wait, come back this way. That is what a family that fears the Lord does. We watch out for each other.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established. -- Proverbs 4:26"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: What does it mean to 'ponder the path of thy feet'?

Number 2: How can we help each other stay on the right path?

Number 3: What are some wrong paths that children and families need to watch for?

Let us pray together. Lord God, we thank You for this time as a family. Thank You for speaking to us through Proverbs chapter 5. Plant these truths deep in our hearts, especially in the hearts of our children. Help us to walk in wisdom tomorrow and every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Goodnight. Remember your memory verse, and carry it with you tomorrow.

[END OF FAMILY DEVOTIONAL, DAY 5]
