PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Walk in Love as Christ Loved Us"
July 20, 2026

---

"Be ye therefore followers of God, as dear children; and walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath given himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour." -- Ephesians 5:1-7

'Followers of God' -- the word is literally 'imitators.' We are to imitate God as children imitate a beloved father. And what is the chief attribute we are to imitate? Love. Not sentimental emotion, but the self-sacrificing love demonstrated at Calvary. Christ gave Himself for us. This is the pattern. This is the measure. This is the standard against which all our love must be tested. The Puritans understood that love without sacrifice is mere sentimentality, and sacrifice without love is mere duty. But love joined to sacrifice -- this is the very heartbeat of the gospel. Jonathan Edwards wrote that true virtue consists in benevolence toward being in general, but its purest expression is in love toward God and toward those whom God loves. Such love is costly. It demands the death of selfishness, the crucifixion of pride, the surrender of personal rights. But it is also glorious, for it reflects the very character of God Himself. When we walk in love, we walk in God's footsteps. When we give ourselves for others, we echo the gift of Christ. Amen.
