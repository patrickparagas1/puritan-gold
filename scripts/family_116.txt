PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 16
"God Directs Our Steps"
Proverbs 16 -- Proverbs 16 -- Family Devotional

---

Good evening, dear family. Welcome to tonight's family devotional from Puritan Gold.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 16. Let me read our key passage:

"A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps."
That is Proverbs 16:9.

Proverbs 16, verse 9 says, A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps. We make plans. That is not wrong. God gave us minds to think and plan. But the beautiful truth of this proverb is that God is the one who actually directs where we end up.

Thomas Watson wrote that this should give us great comfort. We do not have to carry the burden of figuring out our entire future. We plan wisely, we work diligently, and then we trust God with the outcome. He sees the whole picture. We only see a small piece.

Have you ever planned a trip and then something unexpected happened that changed everything? Maybe you got lost and discovered a beautiful place you never would have seen otherwise. That is often how God works. He takes our plans and redirects them for something better than we could have imagined.

As a family, let us hold our plans loosely. Let us make them prayerfully and then trust that God's direction is always better than our own. He knows the way, even when we do not.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"A man's heart deviseth his way: but the LORD directeth his steps. -- Proverbs 16:9"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: What plans has our family made that God changed or directed?

Number 2: How does it feel to know God is guiding your steps?

Number 3: What can we pray about and surrender to God's direction today?

Let us close in prayer. Heavenly Father, thank You for gathering our family together in Your Word tonight. Thank You for the wisdom of Proverbs chapter 16. Help us to live out what we have learned. Guard our hearts, guide our steps, and fill our home with Your love and peace. We pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.

Goodnight, dear ones. Walk in wisdom tomorrow.
