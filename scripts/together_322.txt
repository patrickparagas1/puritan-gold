PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 22
"Training Up Our Children"
Proverbs 22 -- Day 22 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening, dear husband and wife. Welcome to tonight's couples devotional from Puritan Gold. Thank you for setting aside this time to draw near to God and to each other.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 22. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it."
That is Proverbs 22:6.

Train up a child in the way he should go. This is a shared calling for husband and wife. The spiritual formation of your children is not the job of the church alone, nor of one parent alone. It is the sacred work of a mother and father walking together in the fear of the Lord.

Richard Baxter wrote: Richard Baxter, in his Christian Directory, devoted many pages to the sacred duty of training children. He saw it as the most important work any parent could undertake, for the souls of children are entrusted to us by God Himself.

Are you aligned in how you are raising your children spiritually? Do you pray together for your children? Do you model for them what a godly marriage looks like? The greatest gift you can give your children is not a perfect home, but a home where two imperfect people love God and love each other.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

How are we doing at training our children in the Lord? Are we aligned in our approach to spiritual formation at home?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for wisdom in parenting. Ask God to help you train your children together with consistency, love, and faithfulness.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Goodnight, dear couple. May the wisdom of Proverbs chapter 22 take root in your marriage and bear lasting fruit.
