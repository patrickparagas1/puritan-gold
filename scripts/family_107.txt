PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 7
"The Shield of Wisdom"
Proverbs 7 -- Proverbs 7 -- Family Devotional

---

Hello, dear ones. Gather round for our family devotional time.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 7. Let me read our key passage:

"My son, keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee. Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye."
That is Proverbs 7:1-2.

Proverbs chapter 7 is about keeping God's commandments close to us, like a treasured friend. Solomon says, Say unto wisdom, Thou art my sister; and call understanding thy kinswoman. What a beautiful picture. Wisdom should be as close to us as our own family.

Thomas Watson taught that hiding God's Word in our hearts is the single best defense against temptation. When we have memorized Scripture, we carry it with us everywhere we go. It is always there, ready to help us when we face a hard choice.

Think of it like a shield. A soldier would never go into battle without his shield. And God's Word is our shield. When a temptation comes, when a lie whispers in our ear, we can answer it with the truth of Scripture. That is exactly what Jesus did when He was tempted in the wilderness. He answered every temptation with the Word of God.

That is why we memorize our memory verses as a family. We are building our shields together. We are filling our hearts and minds with truth so that when the hard moments come, we are ready.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"Say unto wisdom, Thou art my sister; and call understanding thy kinswoman. -- Proverbs 7:4"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: How is wisdom like a shield or a good friend?

Number 2: Why is it important to know God's Word before temptation comes?

Number 3: How can memorizing Scripture help protect our family?

Let us close in prayer. Heavenly Father, thank You for gathering our family together in Your Word tonight. Thank You for the wisdom of Proverbs chapter 7. Help us to live out what we have learned. Guard our hearts, guide our steps, and fill our home with Your love and peace. We pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.

Sleep well tonight, knowing that God watches over your family.
