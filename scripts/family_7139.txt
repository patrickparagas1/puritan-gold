From "The Mystery of Providence" by John Flavel:

PURITAN GOLD -- The Mystery of Providence: Complete Summary A Comprehensive Overview of John Flavel's Classic Work The Mystery of Providence. By John Flavel. A Complete Summary. Welcome to this summary of John Flavel's The Mystery of Providence, a beloved classic that opens the Christian's eyes to see God's hand in every circumstance of life. Flavel begins by establishing that Providence encompasses everything God does in governing the world and caring for His people. Nothing happens by chance; every event, from the greatest to the smallest, is ordered by the wise and loving hand of our heavenly Father. He traces Providence through the various stages of human life. In our birth and upbringing, God arranges the time, place, and circumstances of our coming into the world. Our parentage, our country, our early influences — all are determined by Providence for purposes we may not understand until eternity. The work of conversion receives special attention. Flavel shows how Providence orchestrates the circumstances that bring the elect to saving faith — the particular sermon heard, the book that fell into our hands, the friend who spoke a timely word, the affliction that broke our pride. In our employment and callings, Providence guides, protects, and provides. Flavel demonstrates how God opens and closes doors, raises up friends and removes enemies, and supplies daily bread through means we often fail to notice. The preservation of the saints is a major theme. Through dangers, temptations, persecutions, and a thousand snares, God keeps His own by His almighty power. Flavel provides numerous examples of remarkable deliverances that can only be explained by divine intervention. He then turns to the duty of meditating upon Providence — a spiritual exercise that Flavel considers essential to healthy Christian living. By keeping a record of God's dealings with us, we strengthen our faith, increase our gratitude, and prepare ourselves for future trials. The advantages of such meditation are manifold: it prevents atheism and unbelief, it supports us in times of affliction, it makes us more thankful for mercies, and it gives us material for worship and praise. Flavel concludes with practical implications and addresses the common difficulties that arise when we try to understand God's ways. He acknowledges that many of God's providences are mysterious and seemingly contradictory, but assures us that all will be made clear in the light of eternity. May this summary encourage you to read the full work and to cultivate the habit of observing and recording God's providences in your own life.
