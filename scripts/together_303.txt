PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 3
"Trusting God with Our Marriage"
Proverbs 3 -- Day 3 -- Couples Devotional

---

Welcome, dear ones. Marriage is a gift from God, and so is this time together in His presence. Let us open our hearts to what He has for us tonight.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 3. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths."
That is Proverbs 3:5-6.

Trust in the Lord with all thine heart. For a married couple, this is both a personal and a shared command. There are moments in every marriage when the path ahead is unclear, when finances are tight, when health is uncertain, when the children bring worry. In those moments, the temptation is to lean on our own understanding, to try to figure it all out ourselves.

Richard Baxter counseled: Richard Baxter, in his Christian Directory, counseled families to begin each day by surrendering their plans to God. He wrote that trust is not passive; it is the active leaning of the whole soul upon the promises of a faithful God.

But God calls us to something better. He calls us to trust Him together. When a husband and wife join hands and say, Lord, we do not know the way, but we trust You, that is one of the most powerful prayers in the world.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

Where do we need to trust God more in our marriage? Are there plans or worries we are holding onto instead of surrendering?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Surrender your plans to God together. Pray over any areas of anxiety or uncertainty and commit them to the Lord.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Until tomorrow, dear ones. May the Lord who brought you together continue to bind your hearts in His love.
