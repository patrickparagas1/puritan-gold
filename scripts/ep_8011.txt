PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Built Upon the Foundation of the Apostles"
July 11, 2026

---

"And came and preached peace to you which were afar off, and to them that were nigh. For through him we both have access by one Spirit unto the Father. Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God; and are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone." -- Ephesians 2:17-22

Paul now reveals the glorious architecture of the church. We are no longer strangers but fellow citizens, no longer foreigners but members of God's household. And this household is built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Jesus Christ Himself as the chief cornerstone. Every stone in this spiritual temple is a living soul, placed by the hand of God in precisely the right position. As William Perkins taught, the church is not a human institution but a divine construction, and its builder and maker is God. The cornerstone determines the alignment of every other stone. Christ sets the standard, the direction, and the character of the whole building. Remove the cornerstone, and the entire structure collapses. But as long as Christ remains, the building stands secure against every storm of persecution, every flood of heresy, and every earthquake of cultural change. And we are being built together -- not as isolated stones piled in a heap, but as a coordinated structure, each stone bearing weight, each stone supporting its neighbor, each stone contributing to the beauty and strength of the whole. This is the church of the living God, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. Amen.
