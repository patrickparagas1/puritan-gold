PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 30
"The Mystery Revealed"
Romans for Marriage — Day 30

---

Hello, beloved couple. It is a beautiful thing when husband and wife sit down together in God's Word. The Puritans called marriage a little church within the church. Tonight, your little church is in session. Welcome.

Tonight we turn to Romans 16:25-27. Listen with your hearts, not just your ears:

"Now to him that is of power to stablish you according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret since the world began, But now is made manifest, and by the scriptures of the prophets, according to the commandment of the everlasting God, made known to all nations for the obedience of faith: To God only wise, be glory through Jesus Christ for ever. Amen."

That is Romans 16:25-27. Take a breath. Let those words settle between the two of you. God is speaking to your marriage through this passage.

--- Puritan Commentary ---

John Owen had deep insight into the truths we have just read. The Puritans did not separate theology from daily life, and their reflections on marriage are remarkably practical.

Paul closes his magisterial letter to the Romans with a doxology that sweeps from eternity past to eternity future. He speaks of a mystery hidden since the world began but now revealed — the mystery of the Gospel, the mystery of God's plan to save sinners from every nation through faith in Jesus Christ.

John Owen, who spent his life plumbing the depths of God's eternal purposes, saw in this doxology the culmination of everything Romans teaches. The God who justified sinners, who sent His Spirit, who works all things together for good, who guarantees inseparable love — this God now establishes believers according to the Gospel. The word 'stablish' means to make firm, to strengthen, to anchor. God does not merely save us and leave us to figure things out. He establishes us — He anchors us firmly in the truth of the Gospel.

Owen applied this to marriage with profound insight. Your marriage is part of God's eternal mystery — part of His grand plan to display the Gospel before all nations. Ephesians 5 explicitly connects marriage to the mystery of Christ and the church. When you love each other sacrificially, you are enacting the mystery. When you forgive each other freely, you are proclaiming the mystery. When you remain faithful through suffering, you are proving the mystery. Your marriage is a window through which the world sees the Gospel.

'Made known to all nations for the obedience of faith' — the mystery is not kept private. It is proclaimed. And your marriage is one of the loudest proclamations. Every neighborhood, every community, every church needs marriages that display the faithfulness, grace, and love of the Gospel. Your marriage is part of God's strategy for reaching the world.

The final phrase says everything: 'To God only wise, be glory through Jesus Christ for ever. Amen.' All glory belongs to God. Your marriage exists for His glory. Every joy, every trial, every victory, every struggle — all of it is meant to bring glory to the only wise God through Jesus Christ. This is the highest purpose your marriage can serve, and it is the deepest satisfaction your marriage will ever know.

--- Marriage Application ---

As you close this month in Romans, receive the closing benediction over your marriage: God is able to establish you. He who began this work will complete it. The mystery of His love has been revealed in Christ, and your marriage is part of that revelation.

You have traveled through thirty days of Romans together. You have explored grace, faith, peace, no condemnation, inseparable love, living sacrifice, genuine affection, and the pursuit of peace. Every truth has been aimed at one goal: that your marriage would glorify God and display the Gospel.

Tonight, rededicate your marriage to God's glory. Not to your comfort, not to your happiness, not to your fulfillment — though all of these will come as byproducts. Dedicate your marriage to the glory of the only wise God through Jesus Christ. Forever. Amen.

The Puritans understood that marriage is not a destination but a journey. Every day presents new opportunities to love, to serve, to forgive, and to grow together. The truths we have studied tonight are not just for tonight. They are for tomorrow morning when the alarm goes off, for the difficult conversation that has been waiting, for the quiet moments when only God sees how you treat each other.

--- Reflection ---

It is time for reflection. Turn to each other and consider these questions together. There is no rush. Let the conversation go wherever the Spirit leads.

Question 1: How has your understanding of marriage deepened through this month's study of Romans?

Question 2: What is the single most important truth God has spoken to your marriage this month?

Question 3: How do you want your marriage to display the Gospel to the world around you in the months ahead?

Take a few minutes with each question. Do not rush. The best conversations in marriage happen when both people feel truly heard.

--- Prayer ---

Now let us pray together. The Puritans said that a couple who prays together is bound by cords that cannot be broken. Let us strengthen those cords tonight.

Tonight's prayer focus: Close with the doxology of Romans 16:25-27 prayed over your marriage: 'Now to Him who is able to establish us according to the Gospel — to God only wise, be glory through Jesus Christ forever.' Dedicate your marriage to His eternal purposes and rest in His power to establish you.

If one of you finds it difficult to pray out loud, that is perfectly fine. Simply holding hands in silence before the Lord is itself a powerful act of worship. Let the Spirit intercede where words fail.

--- Blessing ---

Now to Him who is able to establish your marriage, to keep you faithful, and to display His glory through your love — to the only wise God be glory through Jesus Christ forever. Amen.

The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you both, tonight and always. Goodnight.
