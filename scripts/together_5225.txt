PURITAN GOLD -- Together Devotional, Day 25
"She Opens Her Mouth with Wisdom"
Proverbs 31:26-27 — The Virtuous Wife

---

Good evening, dear husband and wife. Tonight we turn to two verses in the Proverbs 31 portrait that describe the virtuous woman's speech and her watchful care over her household. These are qualities that bless not only the wife but everyone within her sphere of influence.

Let us read our passage. Proverbs 31:26-27 (KJV):

"She openeth her mouth with wisdom; and in her tongue is the law of kindness. She looketh well to the ways of her household, and eateth not the bread of idleness."

--- Teaching ---

Two qualities define the virtuous woman in these verses: wise speech and diligent watchfulness. Together, they paint a picture of a woman who governs her home with both her words and her work.

"She openeth her mouth with wisdom." This is not a woman who speaks carelessly. She does not gossip. She does not complain incessantly. She does not use her words to manipulate, to control, or to wound. When she speaks, what comes out is wisdom, practical, biblical, discerning wisdom that guides her family in the right direction. She is the woman whom her children come to for counsel. She is the woman whom her husband trusts to speak into the most important decisions of their life. Her words carry weight because they are consistently wise.

"And in her tongue is the law of kindness." This is equally remarkable. Not only is her speech wise, but it is kind. The law of kindness governs her tongue, which means kindness is not an occasional quality but a consistent principle. She has disciplined herself to speak with grace. Even her corrections are gentle. Even her disagreements are respectful. Even her firmest words are wrapped in love. William Gouge observed that the combination of wisdom and kindness in speech is exceedingly rare and exceedingly beautiful. Many people are wise but harsh. Others are kind but foolish. The Proverbs 31 woman is both wise and kind, and this combination makes her speech uniquely powerful.

The second quality is diligent watchfulness: "She looketh well to the ways of her household, and eateth not the bread of idleness." The virtuous woman is not passive. She pays attention. She watches over the comings and goings of her household with careful eyes. She notices when something is wrong. She sees needs before they become emergencies. She is proactive rather than reactive. And she does not eat the bread of idleness, which means she is not lazy, not self-indulgent, not content to let things slide while she pursues her own comfort.

Thomas Gataker connected these two qualities. He argued that the wise wife speaks wisely because she watches carefully. Because she pays attention to the ways of her household, she knows what needs to be said and when it needs to be said. Her speech is not theoretical but practical, not abstract but rooted in the daily realities of family life. She does not pontificate from a distance; she counsels from the front lines.

Richard Baxter applied this passage to both husband and wife. While the Proverbs 31 text speaks specifically of the wife, Baxter noted that these virtues are needed in both spouses. The husband, too, should open his mouth with wisdom and let kindness govern his tongue. The husband, too, should look well to the ways of his household and not eat the bread of idleness. The virtuous marriage is one where both partners are engaged, attentive, wise in their speech, and diligent in their work.

For the wife, these verses are both a challenge and an encouragement. They set a high standard, but they also affirm the tremendous value of the work she does every day. The world may not notice the wife who speaks wisely to her children at the breakfast table. The world may not applaud the wife who catches a problem before it becomes a crisis. But God notices. God values this work. And the family that benefits from her wisdom and watchfulness is blessed beyond measure.

Dear couple, let tonight's passage inspire you both. Wife, cultivate wisdom and kindness in your speech. Watch over your household with the attentive love that God has placed in your heart. Husband, honor and support her in this work. Recognize the weight she carries and the wisdom she brings. And together, commit to being a household where wise, kind speech is the norm and where diligent care is the shared responsibility of both partners.

--- For Discussion ---

Question 1: Wife, how do you feel about the quality of your speech within our home? Are there areas where you want to grow in wisdom or kindness?

Question 2: Husband, do you recognize and appreciate the ways your wife watches over the household? How can you better support her in this role?

Question 3: How can we both be more intentional about governing our tongues with the law of kindness?

--- Prayer ---

Gracious Lord, we marvel at the beauty of the virtuous woman who opens her mouth with wisdom and whose tongue is governed by the law of kindness. Grant to the wife in this marriage a growing measure of both wisdom and kindness in her speech. Grant to the husband ears to hear and a heart to honor her wise words. Make us both diligent in watching over the ways of our household, attentive to needs, proactive in care, and faithful in our work. May our home be a place where wisdom reigns and kindness is the language we speak. In Christ's name, Amen.
