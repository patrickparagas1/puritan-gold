The Lord Jesus says, “He that rejects Me, and does not receive My words, has One who judges him” (Joh 12.48), and none can reject Him to whom He is never “offered.” The lengthy quotation which we made above, from one who was a contemporary and intimate friend of the celebrated Jonathan Edwards, contains one of the most lucid and illuminating treatments of that aspect of the theme we are dealing with, which we have ever come across in all our readings. We earnestly commend it to the best attention of interested readers. It throws a flood of light upon the Mosaic economy. It shows how the Everlasting Covenant was being administered, and its blessings bestowed, under the dispensation of the Old Testament.
