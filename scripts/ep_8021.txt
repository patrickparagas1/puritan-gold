PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Walk as Children of Light"
July 21, 2026

---

"For ye were sometimes darkness, but now are ye light in the Lord: walk as children of light: for the fruit of the Spirit is in all goodness and righteousness and truth." -- Ephesians 5:8-14

Note the radical language: Paul does not say we were in darkness but we were darkness itself. Our very nature was dark. But now, through the regenerating work of the Spirit, we are light in the Lord. This is not merely a change of position but a change of nature. As Thomas Goodwin observed, 'God does not merely move us from one room to another; He recreates us as entirely new beings.' And as children of light, we are to produce the fruit of light: goodness, righteousness, and truth. These are not decorations we add to our character; they are the natural produce of a nature that has been transformed by grace. The light reveals what darkness hides. The Christian life is a life of transparency, honesty, and openness before God and before men. We have nothing to hide, for our acceptance is not based on our performance but on the finished work of Christ. Walking in the light means living in reality -- facing the truth about ourselves, about God, and about the world. It means confessing our sins rather than concealing them. It means pursuing righteousness rather than rationalizing compromise. Awake, thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead, and Christ shall give thee light. Amen.
