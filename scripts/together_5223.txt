PURITAN GOLD -- Together Devotional, Day 23
"The Prudent Wife"
Proverbs 19:14 — The Virtuous Wife

---

Good evening, dear husband and wife. Tonight we turn to a brief but beautiful proverb that distinguishes between the gifts that come from human inheritance and the gift that comes directly from the hand of God. Let us listen carefully, for this verse has much to teach us about the value of wisdom in marriage.

Let us read our passage. Proverbs 19:14 (KJV):

"House and riches are the inheritance of fathers: and a prudent wife is from the LORD."

--- Teaching ---

Solomon draws a striking contrast. Houses and riches can be inherited from earthly fathers. They are passed down through family lines, earned through labor, or acquired through the ordinary course of life. But a prudent wife, a wife of wisdom and discernment, comes from a higher source. She is a gift from the Lord.

The word "prudent" in this verse carries a rich range of meaning. It speaks of insight, understanding, good judgment, and the ability to act wisely in practical situations. The prudent wife is not merely intelligent in an academic sense. She is wise in the daily affairs of life. She knows when to speak and when to be silent. She knows when to act and when to wait. She understands people, situations, and seasons. She sees beneath the surface of things and responds with discernment rather than impulse.

Thomas Gataker drew attention to the fact that this prudent wife cannot be purchased or manufactured. She is from the Lord. A man can build a house with his own hands. He can earn riches with his own labor. But he cannot create a prudent wife. She is a work of divine grace. Her wisdom comes from the fear of the Lord, which is the beginning of all true knowledge. Her discernment is the fruit of a life lived in communion with God. When a man finds such a wife, he has found something that no amount of money could buy.

William Gouge connected this passage to the practical life of the household. He observed that the prudent wife is often the quiet engine that keeps the home running smoothly. She manages resources wisely. She anticipates needs before they become crises. She navigates relational tensions with grace. She raises children with a firm and loving hand. She supports her husband's calling without neglecting her own. And she does all of this not for public recognition but for the glory of God and the good of her family.

Gouge also noted that prudence is not a fixed trait but a growing virtue. The wife who seeks wisdom from God's Word, who learns from experience, who submits to godly counsel, and who cultivates self-discipline will grow in prudence over the years. The young wife may lack the seasoned wisdom of the older wife, but if she fears the Lord and pursues His wisdom, she is already on the path of prudence.

For the husband, this passage is a call to deep appreciation. If your wife is a woman of prudence and wisdom, thank God for her. Recognize that her contributions to your home and family are priceless. Do not take her quiet competence for granted. And do not make the mistake of valuing outward qualities like appearance or social charm above the inward quality of godly wisdom. The prudent wife may not draw attention to herself, but she is the most valuable person in your household.

For the wife, this passage is a call to continued growth in wisdom. Seek the Lord daily. Read His Word. Pray for discernment. Learn from older women who have walked this path before you. Proverbs 31:26 says of the virtuous woman: "She openeth her mouth with wisdom; and in her tongue is the law of kindness." This is the prudent wife in action, a woman whose wisdom blesses everyone around her.

Richard Baxter added that the prudent wife is also the wife who brings peace to the home. Where she is present, conflicts are resolved wisely. Decisions are made thoughtfully. The atmosphere is calm rather than chaotic. Her prudence is a stabilizing force that benefits not only her husband but her children, her extended family, and her community.

--- For Discussion ---

Question 1: Husband, in what specific ways has your wife's prudence and wisdom blessed your home and your life?

Question 2: Wife, in what areas of prudence and wisdom would you like to continue growing? How can your husband support that growth?

Question 3: How can we together create an environment in our home where wisdom is valued and cultivated above all external measures of success?

--- Prayer ---

O Lord, the source of all wisdom, we thank Thee for the gift of a prudent wife. Her value is beyond all earthly treasure, for she comes from Thee. Continue to fill her with wisdom, discernment, and understanding. Help the husband to honor her wisdom and to seek her counsel. May our home be governed by the prudence that flows from the fear of the Lord. Grant us both an increasing hunger for Thy Word and Thy ways, that together we might walk in wisdom all the days of our lives. In Jesus' name, Amen.
