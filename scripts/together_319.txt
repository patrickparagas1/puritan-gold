PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 19
"Patience with One Another"
Proverbs 19 -- Day 19 -- Couples Devotional

---

Hello and welcome to your couples devotional. Whether you are sitting side by side on the couch or listening together at the end of a long day, God is here with you both.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 19. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory to pass over a transgression."
That is Proverbs 19:11.

Patience and forgiveness, the two great currencies of a lasting marriage. Proverbs tells us that it is a man's glory to overlook an offense. In marriage, you will have many opportunities to practice this.

Richard Baxter taught: Richard Baxter taught that patience is not weakness but strength under control. He wrote that the man who governs his spirit is greater than he who conquers a city, for self-mastery is the fruit of the Holy Spirit at work.

Forgiveness in marriage is not pretending nothing happened. It is choosing not to hold the offense as a weapon. It is releasing your spouse from the debt of their mistake. And patience is the daily grace that keeps small irritations from becoming large resentments. Practice both, and your marriage will flourish.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

Where do I need more patience with my spouse? Am I quick to forgive or do I hold onto offenses?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for patience and a forgiving spirit. Ask God to help you release offenses quickly and extend grace freely.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Goodnight. Carry the truth of Proverbs chapter 19 into tomorrow, and let it shape the way you love each other.
