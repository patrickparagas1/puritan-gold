PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 19
"A Prudent Partner from the Lord"
Proverbs 19:14

---

Hello and welcome to your couples devotional. Whether you are sitting side by side on the couch or listening together at the end of a long day, God is here with you both. He meets married couples in a special way when they turn their hearts to Him together.

Let me read our passage for this evening. It comes from Proverbs 19:14:

"House and riches are the inheritance of fathers: and a prudent wife is from the LORD."

That is Proverbs 19:14. Take a breath. Let those words settle between the two of you. God is speaking to your marriage through this passage.

--- Puritan Commentary ---

Charles Bridges was a faithful pastor and theologian whose words still carry weight centuries later. Listen to what the Puritans had to say about the truths in tonight's passage.

Charles Bridges highlights the sharp distinction Solomon draws between what can be inherited and what can only be given by God. Houses and riches may pass from generation to generation through earthly means, but a prudent wife is a direct gift of divine providence. Bridges writes: 'Parents may provide an estate for their children, but only God can provide a wise and godly companion for life.'

The word 'prudent' (Hebrew: sakal) carries the meaning of wisdom applied with skill and insight. This is not mere intelligence but wisdom that manifests in practical, relational, and spiritual competence. Bridges notes that prudence in a spouse is a rarer treasure than gold because it cannot be purchased, manufactured, or inherited. It is the work of God's grace in a human soul.

Matthew Henry draws out the implications: if a prudent spouse is from the Lord, then the Lord should be sought in the matter. Henry writes: 'We ought to acknowledge God in the disposal of us and ours in marriage. It is the Lord that made the match, and therefore we must be satisfied with it.' This means that once married, you can trust that God in His providence has given you the partner you have, and that He can work all things for good in your marriage.

Bridges adds a practical note: since prudence is from the Lord, it can also be increased by the Lord. Pray not only for a prudent spouse but for increasing prudence in the spouse you have. Pray for your own prudence. Ask God to deepen the practical wisdom that governs your home, your finances, your parenting, and your relational dynamics.

--- Marriage Application ---

This verse should fill you with both gratitude and hope. Gratitude, because whatever prudence and wisdom your spouse possesses is a gift from God, given specifically to you for your good and His glory. Hope, because the same God who gave this gift can increase it.

Do you pray for your spouse's wisdom to grow? Do you pray for your own? Prudence in marriage manifests in countless ways: in how you handle money, in how you navigate conflict, in how you make decisions about your children, in how you manage your time and energy.

Rather than wishing your spouse were more prudent in certain areas, begin praying specifically for God to grow that prudence. And examine yourself: in what areas do you need greater wisdom? Ask for it. James 1:5 promises that God gives wisdom liberally to all who ask.

The Puritans understood that marriage is not a destination but a journey. Every day presents new opportunities to love, to serve, to forgive, and to grow together. The truths we have studied tonight are not just for tonight. They are for tomorrow morning when the alarm goes off, for the difficult conversation that has been waiting, for the quiet moments when only God sees how you treat each other.

--- Reflection ---

Let us reflect together. These questions are designed to help you go deeper, both with God and with each other.

Question 1: In what specific ways has God's gift of my spouse's prudence blessed our life together?

Question 2: What areas of our life together most need greater prudence, and are we praying for it?

Question 3: How does recognizing my spouse as God's gift change the way I view our differences and challenges?

Take a few minutes with each question. Do not rush. The best conversations in marriage happen when both people feel truly heard.

--- Prayer ---

As we near the end of tonight's devotional, let us bring our hearts before God in prayer. Pray for each other. Pray for your marriage. Pray for your home.

Tonight's prayer focus: Thank God for the prudence He has already given your spouse, and ask Him to increase it. Pray for wisdom in specific areas of your life together. Ask God for the humility to see your spouse as His carefully chosen gift to you.

If one of you finds it difficult to pray out loud, that is perfectly fine. Simply holding hands in silence before the Lord is itself a powerful act of worship. Let the Spirit intercede where words fail.

--- Blessing ---

May the Lord who gives prudence as a gift from heaven continue to pour wisdom into your marriage, making you both skillful stewards of every blessing He entrusts to your care. Amen.

The Lord bless you and keep you. The Lord make His face shine upon you and be gracious to you. The Lord lift up His countenance upon you both, and give you peace. Amen.
