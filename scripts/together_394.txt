PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 2
"Love Her as Christ Loved"
Ephesians 5:25 — The Husband's Supreme Calling

---

Welcome back, dear couple. Last night we considered the calling of the wife in Ephesians 5. Tonight we turn to what is perhaps the most demanding command in all of Scripture directed at husbands. It is a command that should make every husband tremble — and then fall on his knees in gratitude that the same Christ who issues the command also supplies the grace to obey it.

Our Scripture tonight is Ephesians 5:25:

"Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it."

One verse. Fourteen words in the King James. And yet an entire lifetime is not enough to plumb the depths of this calling.

--- Teaching ---

The Puritan Thomas Gataker, in his beautiful treatise on marriage, observed that Paul spends far more words instructing husbands than wives in this passage. Three verses address the wife's calling; twelve verses address the husband's. The reason is simple: the husband bears the greater weight of responsibility. His calling is not to command, but to die.

"Even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it." The standard is not cultural convention. The standard is not what other husbands do. The standard is Christ. And what did Christ do? He gave Himself. He emptied Himself. He took the form of a servant. He went to the cross. He bore the sin, the shame, the agony, the separation from His Father — all for the sake of His Bride.

William Gouge wrote that a husband who understands this verse will never need to demand respect from his wife. A man who daily lays down his life for his wife — who puts her needs above his own, who sacrifices his comfort for her flourishing, who guards her heart as his most precious treasure — such a man will find that respect flows naturally from his wife's heart, not because he has demanded it, but because he has earned it through Christlike love.

The Puritans were remarkably practical in their application of this verse. Gouge listed specific ways a husband should love his wife: he should speak tenderly to her, defend her reputation, provide for her needs, consult her in decisions, bear with her weaknesses, and never use harsh or demeaning words. He should be her greatest encourager, her closest friend, and her most faithful protector.

Richard Baxter added that a husband must love his wife with patience, recognizing that she, like himself, is a sinner in need of grace. "Do not expect perfection from your wife," Baxter counseled, "for you do not offer it yourself." Instead, love her as Christ loves the Church — with full knowledge of her faults and a firm commitment to her good.

This is radical love. This is love that does not keep score. This is love that does not withdraw when it is inconvenient or painful. This is love that looks at the cross and says, "That is my model. That is my standard. That is the love I am called to give."

Husband, hear this: your wife is not your servant. She is your bride. She is the one for whom you are called to lay down your life — daily, gladly, sacrificially. And the beautiful paradox of the Gospel is this: in losing your life for her sake, you will find it. In serving her, you will discover a joy that self-centered living can never produce.

And wife, hear this as well: when your husband stumbles — and he will — extend to him the same grace that Christ extends to you. Encourage him in his calling. Remind him of the man God is shaping him to be. Your patience and your prayers are powerful instruments of sanctification.

--- Reflection Prompts ---

Turn to each other and consider these questions:

Question 1: Husband, in what specific areas of daily life can you more fully imitate Christ's sacrificial love for your wife?

Question 2: Wife, how can you encourage your husband in his calling to Christlike love without placing impossible expectations on him?

Question 3: Together, what does "laying down your life" look like in the ordinary rhythms of your week — in the kitchen, the bedroom, the budget, the schedule?

--- Prayer Focus ---

Pray together tonight for the husband's calling. Ask God to give every husband who hears this the grace to love his wife as Christ loves the Church. Pray that the standard of the cross would replace the standard of the culture, and that sacrificial love would become the defining mark of your marriage.

--- Closing ---

May the love of Christ, who gave Himself for His Bride, fill your marriage with the same self-giving joy. May you grow in tenderness, in patience, and in sacrificial devotion. The peace of God, which passeth all understanding, keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus. Goodnight.
