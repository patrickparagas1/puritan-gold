PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"The God of All Grace"
July 31, 2026

---

"But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you. To him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen." -- 1 Peter 5:10-11

We close this month with Peter's benediction, which gathers up all the themes we have explored in Ephesians and applies them to the suffering, struggling believer. The God of all grace -- not some grace, not occasional grace, but all grace for all needs at all times. He has called us unto His eternal glory -- this is our destination, our inheritance, our certain hope. After that ye have suffered a while -- the suffering is real, but it is temporary. It is 'a while,' not forever. And the four verbs that follow are God's promises to every child of His: He will make you perfect -- complete, mature, lacking nothing. He will establish you -- make you firm, steady, unshakeable. He will strengthen you -- infuse you with divine power for every trial. He will settle you -- plant you on a foundation that cannot be moved. As Thomas Brooks wrote, 'God's people may be bruised reeds, but He will not break them. They may be smoking flax, but He will not quench them. He will fan the spark into a flame and strengthen the reed into an oak.' To Him be glory and dominion forever. This has been our theme all month, and it shall be our song for eternity. The God who began a good work in us will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ. Soli Deo Gloria. Amen.
