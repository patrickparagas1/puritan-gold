PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"The Mystery of Christ Revealed"
July 12, 2026

---

"For this cause I Paul, the prisoner of Jesus Christ for you Gentiles, if ye have heard of the dispensation of the grace of God which is given me to you-ward: how that by revelation he made known unto me the mystery; as I wrote afore in few words." -- Ephesians 3:1-7

Paul calls himself 'the prisoner of Jesus Christ,' not the prisoner of Rome. Though chains bound his wrists, his spirit was free, for he understood that even his imprisonment was part of God's sovereign plan for the spread of the gospel. The mystery he speaks of is that Gentiles are fellow heirs with Israel, members of the same body, sharers in the same promise. This was hidden in ages past but now revealed by the Spirit. John Owen observed that God's method of revelation is progressive -- He unfolds His purposes gradually, like the dawn that grows brighter and brighter unto the perfect day. The inclusion of the Gentiles was always in the plan, foreshadowed in Abraham's call to be a blessing to all nations, but it was not fully understood until the Spirit illuminated the apostles. What a wonder that God would use a former persecutor of the church as the chief apostle to the nations! This is grace upon grace -- not only saving Paul from his sins but commissioning him to proclaim the very gospel he once sought to destroy. As Augustine said, 'God does not merely forgive the sinner; He transforms him into a saint and then makes him an apostle.' Amen.
