PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 13
"Choosing Wise Companions"
Proverbs 13 -- Day 13 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening, dear couple. The Puritans believed that marriage was one of the greatest means of grace God provides. Let us enter into that grace together tonight.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 13. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed."
That is Proverbs 13:20.

The friends you keep as a couple will shape the kind of marriage you build. Proverbs warns that a companion of fools shall be destroyed, but he who walks with the wise shall be wise. This applies powerfully to marriage.

Richard Baxter counseled: Richard Baxter strongly counseled young people to choose their companions with great care. He wrote that the friends you walk with will shape the person you become, for the influence of daily fellowship is stronger than we know.

Surround your marriage with couples who love the Lord. Seek out mentors, older and wiser couples who have walked the road before you. Avoid relationships that undermine your commitment or tempt you away from faithfulness. The community around your marriage matters more than you know.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

Do the people we spend the most time with build up or weaken our marriage? Are there friendships we should cultivate or boundaries we should set?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for wise friends and mentors. Ask God to surround your marriage with couples who love Him and will encourage your faithfulness.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

May the God of all grace strengthen your marriage tonight and in the days to come. Goodnight, dear husband and wife.
