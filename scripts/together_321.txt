PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 21
"God Sees Our Hearts"
Proverbs 21 -- Day 21 -- Couples Devotional

---

Welcome, husband and wife. There is something powerful about two hearts turning toward God at the same time. Let us do that now, together.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 21. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts."
That is Proverbs 21:2.

God ponders the hearts. He sees past our outward displays of affection to the true motives underneath. In marriage, it is possible to go through the motions, to say the right words and do the right things, while our hearts are far away.

Jonathan Edwards taught: Jonathan Edwards taught that true religion is not about outward conformity but about the affections of the heart. God weighs not merely our actions but the motives and desires that drive them.

Tonight, let God search your heart. Are you serving your spouse out of genuine love, or out of obligation? Are you present in your marriage, or just physically there? God wants not just your actions but your heart. Give it to Him afresh tonight, and ask Him to fill it with true love for your spouse.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

If God weighed our hearts today, what would He find? Are we serving each other with pure motives?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Ask God to search your hearts as a couple. Pray for purity of motive, sincerity of love, and freedom from selfishness.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

The Lord bless you and keep you both. May He make His face shine upon your marriage. Goodnight.
