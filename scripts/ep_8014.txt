PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Strengthened with Might in the Inner Man"
July 14, 2026

---

"For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named, that he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man; that Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love, may be able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height; and to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge, that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God." -- Ephesians 3:14-19

Here is the second great prayer of Ephesians, and it is even more staggering than the first. Paul prays that we might know the love of Christ 'which passeth knowledge' -- a love so vast that it surpasses the capacity of the human mind to comprehend it. It has breadth enough to encompass all nations and peoples. It has length enough to stretch from eternity past to eternity future. It has depth enough to reach the lowest sinner in the deepest pit. It has height enough to raise that sinner to the throne of God. Samuel Rutherford, writing from his exile in Aberdeen, poured out his heart in language that burns with holy passion: 'O love of Christ! O exceeding, boundless love! I cannot reach it, I cannot fathom it, I am swallowed up in its immensity.' The ultimate purpose of this prayer is breathtaking: 'that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God.' Not merely touched by God, not merely influenced by God, but filled with all the fullness of the infinite, eternal God. This is our destiny, beloved. This is what we were made for. And the Spirit of God is working in us even now to bring it to pass. Amen.
