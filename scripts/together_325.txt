PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 25
"Overcoming Evil with Good"
Proverbs 25 -- Day 25 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening. What a blessing it is to see a husband and wife seeking the Lord together. That is exactly what this time is for. Let us begin.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 25. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he be thirsty, give him water to drink."
That is Proverbs 25:21.

If thine enemy be hungry, give him bread to eat. This teaching about overcoming evil with good applies within marriage too. There are moments when your spouse may feel like the enemy, when hurt and anger make love feel impossible. In those moments, choose kindness anyway.

Jeremiah Burroughs taught: Jeremiah Burroughs taught that responding to enemies with kindness is the most powerful testimony a Christian can give. It is not natural; it is supernatural. It is the love of Christ flowing through a surrendered heart.

The love that overcomes is not a feeling. It is a decision. It is choosing to serve when you would rather withdraw. It is choosing to speak kindly when you want to lash out. This is the supernatural love that only the Holy Spirit can produce in us.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

Is there someone who has hurt us or our family? How can we respond with kindness and forgiveness as a couple?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for those who have wronged you. Ask God for the grace to overcome evil with good, together as one.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Rest well in the Lord tonight. He is faithful to your marriage, and His mercies are new every morning.
