PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 20
"Building a Good Name Together"
Proverbs 20 -- Day 20 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening, dear couple. The Puritans believed that marriage was one of the greatest means of grace God provides. Let us enter into that grace together tonight.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 20. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right."
That is Proverbs 20:11.

Even a child is known by his doings. As a couple, you are building a legacy, a reputation, a family name. Not in the worldly sense, but in the eternal sense. What kind of legacy will your marriage leave?

Thomas Watson reminded us: Thomas Watson reminded parents that children are known by their deeds from their earliest years. The habits formed in childhood become the character of the adult. Therefore, train them in righteousness from the very first.

Your children are watching. Your neighbors are watching. The world is watching. A marriage that reflects the love of Christ is one of the most powerful witnesses on earth. Live in such a way that your marriage points others to the gospel.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

What kind of legacy are we building together? How do others see our marriage? Does it point them to Christ?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for a godly legacy. Ask God to help your marriage reflect His love and faithfulness to everyone who sees it.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

May the God of all grace strengthen your marriage tonight and in the days to come. Goodnight, dear husband and wife.
