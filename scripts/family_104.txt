PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 4
"Guarding Our Hearts"
Proverbs 4 -- Proverbs 4 -- Family Devotional

---

Good evening, beloved family. Let us draw near to God together.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 4. Let me read our key passage:

"Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life."
That is Proverbs 4:23.

Keep thy heart with all diligence, Solomon writes in Proverbs chapter 4. Your heart is like a garden. Whatever seeds you plant in it will grow. If you plant seeds of kindness, truth, and love for God, beautiful things will grow. But if you let weeds of anger, lies, or selfishness take root, they will choke out the good things.

That is why God tells us to guard our hearts. He knows that what goes into our hearts through our eyes and ears eventually comes out in our words and actions. The things we watch, the music we listen to, the conversations we have, the thoughts we think, they all plant seeds.

Thomas Brooks, a Puritan preacher who wrote a famous book about how to fight temptation, warned that the devil's favorite strategy is to sneak bad things into our hearts when we are not paying attention. He does not knock loudly on the front door. He slips in through a window we left open. That is why guarding our hearts is so important.

So how do we guard our hearts? By filling them with good things! When we read the Bible, sing hymns, pray, and speak kindly to one another, we are planting good seeds and locking the windows against the enemy. Let us be a family that guards our hearts together.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life. -- Proverbs 4:23"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: What kinds of things go into our hearts through what we watch and listen to?

Number 2: Why does the Bible say our heart is so important?

Number 3: What is one thing we can change this week to better guard our hearts?

Let us close in prayer. Heavenly Father, thank You for gathering our family together in Your Word tonight. Thank You for the wisdom of Proverbs chapter 4. Help us to live out what we have learned. Guard our hearts, guide our steps, and fill our home with Your love and peace. We pray in the name of Jesus Christ. Amen.

Rest well tonight. God is faithful, and His wisdom is sure.
