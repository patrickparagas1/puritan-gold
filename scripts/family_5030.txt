PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 30
"A Benediction for Summer"
Summer Synthesis — Carrying Puritan Wisdom into Summer Family Rhythms

---

Good evening, dear family. Welcome to our final Puritan Gold family devotional for June. Tonight we close this month with a benediction — a sending forth — as your family steps into the fullness of summer with hearts shaped by Puritan wisdom and sustained by the grace of God. Let us begin with prayer.

Let us pray together. Father, we come before You with grateful hearts. You have fed us richly this month with the wisdom of Your servants and the truth of Your Word. As we close this chapter and open the next, bless our family and send us forth with Your peace. In Christ's name, Amen.

Our Scripture reading this evening comes from Numbers 6:24-26 and Jude 24-25. Let us hear the Word of the Lord:

"The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy, To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen."

That is Numbers 6:24-26 and Jude 24-25. Let those words settle in your heart for a moment before we continue.

--- Teaching ---

A benediction is not merely a nice ending to a service. It is a divine blessing — a word of power spoken over God's people as they go out into the world. The Aaronic benediction in Numbers 6 was spoken over Israel as they prepared to journey through the wilderness, and it carried the authority and love of God Himself. Tonight, this benediction is spoken over your family as you journey into summer.

"The Lord bless thee, and keep thee." This is a prayer for God's active, generous goodness to rest upon your family and for His protective power to surround you. As you travel, as you play, as you rest, as you work — may the Lord bless you with His presence and keep you from every evil.

"The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee." The shining face of God is the face of favor, delight, and pleasure. This is a prayer that God would look upon your family with joy — that He would take pleasure in you, not because of your perfection, but because of His grace. May you feel the warmth of His smile upon your family this summer.

"The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace." The lifted countenance is the face of attention and care. God does not look away. He does not forget. He lifts His face toward your family and gives you peace — not the fragile peace of favorable circumstances, but the deep, abiding peace that comes from knowing that the sovereign God of the universe is attentive to your family's every need.

As we close, let me briefly recall the four great truths we have gathered this month and send your family forth with each one.

From Thomas Watson, go forth with the law of God written on your hearts. Let the Ten Commandments not be mere rules but the architecture of a beautiful family life — a life of worship, reverence, rest, honor, kindness, faithfulness, honesty, and contentment.

From John Flavel, go forth with trust in God's providence. Whatever this summer holds — the expected and the unexpected, the joyful and the difficult — know that nothing happens outside of God's sovereign care. He is writing your family's story, and every chapter serves His good purpose.

From Jonathan Edwards, go forth with holy affections aflame. Do not let your love for God grow cold in the ease of summer. Fan the flame through worship, through wonder at creation, through the daily practice of turning your hearts toward the beauty of God.

From Jeremiah Burroughs, go forth with the rare jewel of contentment nestled in your hearts. Whether your summer is full of adventure or full of ordinary days, whether your budget is generous or tight, whether your plans succeed or fail — be content. God Himself is your portion, and He is more than enough.

And from Jude, go forth with confidence. The God who is able to keep you from falling — this God goes with you into summer. He will present you faultless before His glory with exceeding joy. Not because you are faultless, but because Christ's righteousness covers you completely. Your family's future is secure in His hands.

This has been a sacred month. Thirty evenings of sitting together, opening the Scriptures, hearing from the Puritan masters, discussing truth, and praying as a family. These evenings are seeds planted in the soil of your children's hearts, and they will bear fruit for years and decades to come. Do not underestimate what God has done in these quiet moments around the family table.

Go in peace, dear family. The Lord goes before you and behind you, above you and beneath you, within you and around you. He is your God, and you are His people. And nothing in all creation can separate you from His love.

--- Discussion Questions ---

Let us pause and talk as a family. Here are tonight's discussion questions. Everyone gets a turn to share.

Question 1: As we look back over this entire month, what is the single truth or moment that stands out most to each family member?

Question 2: How has our family changed through these thirty days of devotion together? What growth can we see?

Question 3: What commitment do we want to make as a family for the summer ahead — a practice, a prayer, a verse, or a habit that will keep us growing?

Take a few minutes to talk about these questions together. Let everyone share, and listen to one another with patience and love.

--- Memory Verse ---

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace." -- Numbers 6:24-26

Try to say it out loud three times right now. Then, before bed tonight, see if you can say it from memory.

--- Closing ---

Let us pray together. Lord God, we thank You for this month. Thank You for Watson, Flavel, Edwards, and Burroughs. Thank You for Your Word that endures forever. Thank You for the gift of family. Now send us forth into summer with Your blessing upon us. Keep us. Shine Your face upon us. Be gracious to us. Give us peace. And bring us back together for another month of Your truth. In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, Amen.

Sleep well tonight, blessed by the God who will never leave you nor forsake you. Happy summer, dear family.
