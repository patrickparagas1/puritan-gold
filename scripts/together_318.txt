PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 18
"Running to the Strong Tower"
Proverbs 18 -- Day 18 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening. What a blessing it is to see a husband and wife seeking the Lord together. That is exactly what this time is for. Let us begin.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 18. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe."
That is Proverbs 18:10.

The name of the Lord is a strong tower. When storms hit your marriage, and they will, where do you run? Do you run to anger, to distraction, to blame? Or do you run together to the strong tower of the Lord?

Thomas Brooks wrote: Thomas Brooks wrote that the name of the Lord encompasses all His perfections, His power, His mercy, His faithfulness, His love. When the righteous run into this tower, they find not merely safety but rest for the soul.

The couples who endure are the ones who pray together in crisis. Not perfectly, not with eloquent words, but honestly. Lord, we are scared. Lord, we do not know what to do. Lord, be our strong tower. That kind of prayer, spoken together, is stronger than any storm.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

When trouble comes, do we run to God together or try to handle it alone? How can we make prayer our first response?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray together over any current storms or challenges. Run to the Strong Tower together and find your safety in Him.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Rest well in the Lord tonight. He is faithful to your marriage, and His mercies are new every morning.
