PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 31
"A Marriage That Fears the Lord"
Proverbs 31 -- Day 31 -- Couples Devotional

---

Welcome, dear ones. Marriage is a gift from God, and so is this time together in His presence. Let us open our hearts to what He has for us tonight.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 31. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: but a woman that feareth the LORD, she shall be praised."
That is Proverbs 31:30.

Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: but a woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised. As we close this month of devotions together, let us reflect on what truly matters. The world chases after charm and beauty, but these things fade. What lasts is the fear of the Lord.

Thomas Watson wrote beautifully: Thomas Watson wrote beautifully about the fear of the Lord as the crowning virtue of a godly life. All outward graces fade, but the soul that fears the Lord possesses an imperishable beauty that grows brighter unto the perfect day.

A marriage rooted in the fear of God is a marriage that will bear fruit in every season. The spring of young love, the summer of raising children, the autumn of the empty nest, the winter of old age, through it all, the fear of the Lord sustains, strengthens, and sweetens every chapter of your story together.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

As we close this month, what has God taught us about our marriage? How has the fear of the Lord shaped our love for each other?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray a prayer of thanksgiving for your marriage. Commit to continuing to fear the Lord together and to building on the foundation of wisdom.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Until tomorrow, dear ones. May the Lord who brought you together continue to bind your hearts in His love.
