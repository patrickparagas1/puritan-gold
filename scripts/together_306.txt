PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 6
"Working Together with Diligence"
Proverbs 6 -- Day 6 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening, dear couple. The Puritans believed that marriage was one of the greatest means of grace God provides. Let us enter into that grace together tonight.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 6. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise: Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler, Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest."
That is Proverbs 6:6-8.

The ant works diligently without needing anyone to tell her what to do. In marriage, this principle is transformative. A strong marriage is built not in dramatic moments but in the faithful, daily work of two people who love each other enough to serve without being asked.

Richard Baxter taught: Richard Baxter urged believers to be diligent stewards of their time. In his Saints' Everlasting Rest, he wrote that the diligent soul finds God in labor, in rest, and in every duty. The sluggard loses not only time but communion with God.

When a husband serves his wife without waiting to be asked, when a wife encourages her husband without expecting anything in return, when both partners contribute to the household with cheerful diligence, the home becomes a place of warmth and strength. Small acts of service, done consistently, build an unshakable foundation.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

Are we working together diligently to build our home and family? Where might laziness or neglect be creeping in?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for a diligent spirit in your home. Ask God to help you serve each other and your family with cheerful, consistent effort.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

May the God of all grace strengthen your marriage tonight and in the days to come. Goodnight, dear husband and wife.
