PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"The Belt of Truth and the Breastplate of Righteousness"
July 26, 2026

---

"Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness." -- Ephesians 6:14

The first piece of armor is truth. A Roman soldier's belt held everything together; without it, his tunic would flap loose and his sword would have nothing to hang from. So truth is the foundation of the Christian's defense. Truth about God, truth about ourselves, truth about sin and salvation, truth about the promises of Scripture. When Satan attacks, he begins with lies -- questioning God's word, distorting God's character, minimizing sin, exaggerating our failures. The man who is girded with truth can recognize and reject every lie. The breastplate of righteousness protects the heart -- and here it refers not only to the imputed righteousness of Christ that gives us our standing before God, but also the practical righteousness of daily obedience that gives us confidence in the heat of battle. As John Owen taught, the believer who tolerates known sin in his life has a gap in his armor that the enemy will exploit without mercy. Pursue holiness, for the breastplate of righteousness is your protection against the accusing darts of the evil one. Amen.
