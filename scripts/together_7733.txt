Many Scriptures If it be true that God loves every member of the human family, then why did our Lord tell His disciples, “He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father...If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him” (Joh 14:21, 23)? Why say, “he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father,” if the Father loves everybody? The same limitation is found in Proverbs 8:17: “I love them that love me.” Again, we read, “Thou hatest all workers of iniquity”—not merely the works of iniquity.
