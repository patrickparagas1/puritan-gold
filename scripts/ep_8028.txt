PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Praying Always with All Prayer"
July 28, 2026

---

"Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints; and for me, that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel." -- Ephesians 6:18-20

Prayer is the atmosphere in which the whole armor of God functions. Without prayer, the armor is merely a museum exhibit -- impressive to look at but useless in battle. The Puritans were, above all else, men and women of prayer. They prayed in their closets, at their tables, in their churches, and in their marketplaces. They prayed morning, noon, and night. They prayed in prosperity and in persecution. John Bradford, the Puritan martyr, was said to have spent so much time on his knees that they grew callused. Matthew Henry's father, Philip Henry, would pray for hours at a time, weeping over the sins of the nation and the needs of the church. Paul asks for prayer not for his comfort or release from prison, but for boldness in preaching. This reveals the heart of a true minister -- his greatest concern is not his own welfare but the advance of the gospel. As Richard Baxter wrote, 'I preached as a dying man to dying men, as never sure to preach again.' May we all catch that sense of urgency, that holy boldness, that consuming passion for the souls of men and the glory of God. Amen.
