PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 29
"A Shared Vision for Your Marriage"
Proverbs 29:18

---

Good evening, dear husband and wife. Welcome to tonight's couples devotional from Puritan Gold. Thank you for setting aside this time to draw near to God and to each other. The moments you invest in your marriage before the Lord are among the most important moments of your day.

Let us open God's Word together. Our passage tonight comes from Proverbs 29:18:

"Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he."

That is Proverbs 29:18. Take a breath. Let those words settle between the two of you. God is speaking to your marriage through this passage.

--- Puritan Commentary ---

Now let us hear from Charles Bridges, one of the great Puritan voices on this subject. The Puritans wrote extensively about marriage because they saw it as one of God's most profound gifts.

Charles Bridges is careful to establish the meaning of 'vision' here. This is not entrepreneurial vision or personal ambition; the Hebrew word (chazon) refers to prophetic revelation: the Word of God communicated to His people. Where there is no Word of God, the people 'perish' — or more literally, 'cast off restraint.' They run wild, like sheep without a shepherd.

Bridges writes: 'The vision is not man's dream but God's revelation. Without it, there is no direction, no standard, no foundation for life. The people are not merely unhappy; they are undone.' The second clause provides the contrast: the one who keeps the law, who orders life by God's revealed will, is 'happy' — blessed, fulfilled, secure.

Matthew Henry adds that this verse speaks to the necessity of regular, sustained exposure to God's Word. A church without faithful preaching will deteriorate. A nation without moral law will descend into chaos. And a family without the Word of God as its guiding vision will drift, fragment, and ultimately dissolve.

Henry applies this with urgency: 'Where there is no preaching, no reading of the Word, no family worship, the people go to ruin. But where the Bible is opened, read, believed, and obeyed, there is order, peace, and blessing.' Bridges concurs, adding that 'the marriage without a shared vision grounded in God's Word is a ship without a rudder, at the mercy of every cultural current.'

--- Marriage Application ---

Does your marriage have a shared vision? Not merely shared goals (a bigger house, a better vacation, financial security) but a shared vision rooted in God's Word: What is God calling us to as a couple? What kind of family is He building through us? What is our purpose in His kingdom?

A marriage without vision will drift. You will wake up one day and realize that you have been pursuing separate agendas, moving in different directions, building toward different ends. But a marriage grounded in God's revealed will — in the vision of His Word — will have direction, purpose, and the deep happiness that comes from living in alignment with the Creator's design.

Take time to articulate your shared vision. What Scriptures define your marriage? What values guide your decisions? What legacy do you want to leave? Write it down. Revisit it regularly. Let it be the rudder of your ship.

The Puritans understood that marriage is not a destination but a journey. Every day presents new opportunities to love, to serve, to forgive, and to grow together. The truths we have studied tonight are not just for tonight. They are for tomorrow morning when the alarm goes off, for the difficult conversation that has been waiting, for the quiet moments when only God sees how you treat each other.

--- Reflection ---

Now let us pause for reflection. Here are some questions for you and your spouse to discuss together. Be honest. Be gentle. Listen more than you speak.

Question 1: Do we have a clear, shared vision for our marriage that is grounded in God's Word?

Question 2: If we do not have such a vision, what steps can we take this week to begin developing one?

Question 3: How has the absence of vision, or the presence of it, affected the direction of our marriage so far?

Take a few minutes with each question. Do not rush. The best conversations in marriage happen when both people feel truly heard.

--- Prayer ---

Let us move into a time of prayer together. If you are able, hold hands as you pray. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Tonight's prayer focus: Ask God to give your marriage a vision that is rooted in His Word. Pray for clarity about His purposes for your family, your ministry together, and your legacy. Ask for the discipline to keep His law and thereby find true happiness.

If one of you finds it difficult to pray out loud, that is perfectly fine. Simply holding hands in silence before the Lord is itself a powerful act of worship. Let the Spirit intercede where words fail.

--- Blessing ---

May the Lord who reveals His will through His Word give your marriage a vision that never fades, a purpose that endures, and a joy that overflows. Amen.

May the Lord bless your marriage and keep it strong. May He make His face to shine upon your home, and give you peace. Goodnight, dear couple. Walk together in His love.
