PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 29
"Holding to the Vision"
Proverbs 29 -- Day 29 -- Couples Devotional

---

Good evening, dear husband and wife. Welcome to tonight's couples devotional from Puritan Gold. Thank you for setting aside this time to draw near to God and to each other.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 29. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he."
That is Proverbs 29:18.

Where there is no vision, the people perish. Does your marriage have a vision? Not just a plan for next year's vacation, but a God-given vision for what your marriage and family are meant to be and do for His kingdom?

Richard Baxter wrote: Richard Baxter lamented the spiritual blindness that comes when people cast off the authority of God's Word. Without the vision of Scripture, he wrote, men wander in darkness and perish for lack of direction.

Take time to dream together under the authority of God's Word. What is He calling your marriage to? What ministries, what service, what legacy? A marriage with a shared, God-given vision is a marriage with purpose and power.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

Do we have a shared vision for our marriage rooted in God's Word? What is God calling us to build together?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for vision. Ask God to give you a clear picture of what He is calling your marriage to be and to do for His kingdom.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Goodnight, dear couple. May the wisdom of Proverbs chapter 29 take root in your marriage and bear lasting fruit.
