PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Grieve Not the Holy Spirit"
July 19, 2026

---

"And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption. Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice: and be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you." -- Ephesians 4:25-32

'Grieve not the Holy Spirit.' What a tender expression! The Spirit is not an impersonal force but a divine Person who can be grieved. And what grieves Him? Not primarily the great sins that shock the world, but the everyday sins of the tongue and the heart -- bitterness, wrath, anger, clamour, evil speaking, malice. These are the sins that poison relationships, divide churches, and quench the Spirit's work in our midst. Thomas Watson wrote, 'An unkind word from a believer's tongue does more damage to the cause of Christ than a thousand blasphemies from the world.' How sobering. The antidote is not merely restraint but transformation: be kind, tenderhearted, forgiving -- even as God for Christ's sake hath forgiven you. The measure of our forgiveness toward others is the measure of God's forgiveness toward us. If we truly grasp how much we have been forgiven, we will find it impossible to hold grudges against those who have wronged us. As the Puritan William Gurnall taught, 'He who has been forgiven a debt of ten thousand talents will not strangle his brother for a hundred pence.' Amen.
