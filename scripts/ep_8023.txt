PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Wives and Husbands, Christ and the Church"
July 23, 2026

---

"Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord. Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it." -- Ephesians 5:22-33

Paul takes the most intimate of human relationships and sets it against the backdrop of the most glorious of divine realities: the love of Christ for His church. Marriage, he tells us, is not merely a social institution or a legal contract; it is a living picture of the gospel. The husband is called to love his wife as Christ loved the church -- which means laying down his life for her, nourishing her, cherishing her, seeking her highest good at the cost of his own comfort. As Matthew Henry beautifully wrote, 'The woman was not made out of man's head to rule over him, nor out of his feet to be trampled upon by him, but out of his side to be equal with him, under his arm to be protected, and near his heart to be loved.' The wife is called to submit as the church submits to Christ -- not as a slave to a tyrant, but as a beloved bride to a loving Savior who has given everything for her. Richard Baxter, in his 'Christian Directory,' counseled couples to see their marriage as a small church within the larger church, a place where the gospel is lived out daily in the thousand small acts of love, patience, forgiveness, and self-sacrifice that make up the fabric of married life. This is the great mystery, Paul says -- he is speaking of Christ and the church. Amen.
