PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Children and Parents in the Lord"
July 24, 2026

---

"Children, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is right. Honour thy father and mother; which is the first commandment with promise; that it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth. And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord." -- Ephesians 6:1-4

The Christian household is a school of godliness, and the parent-child relationship is one of its primary classrooms. Children are commanded to obey -- not because parents are always right, but because this is the order God has established for the nurture and protection of those who are not yet mature enough to guide themselves. Cotton Mather, that prodigious Puritan of New England, wrote extensively on the duty of parents to catechize their children, to pray with them morning and evening, and to set before them by daily example what it means to follow Christ. He taught that the home is the first church, the first school, and the first government a child will ever know, and that the habits formed in childhood will shape the entire course of a person's life. But Paul also speaks to fathers: 'Provoke not your children to wrath.' This is a warning against harsh, unreasonable, arbitrary, or cruel discipline that crushes the spirit rather than shaping the character. As Jonathan Edwards practiced in his own home, the goal of Christian parenting is not mere obedience but the cultivation of genuine faith, love for God, and a heart that delights in righteousness. Bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord -- with tenderness and instruction, with discipline and love, with firmness and grace. Amen.
