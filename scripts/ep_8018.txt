PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Put Off the Old Man, Put On the New"
July 18, 2026

---

"This I say therefore, and testify in the Lord, that ye henceforth walk not as other Gentiles walk, in the vanity of their mind, having the understanding darkened, being alienated from the life of God through the ignorance that is in them, because of the blindness of their heart." -- Ephesians 4:17-24

The Christian life is a daily exchange -- putting off the old man with his corrupt practices and putting on the new man, created in righteousness and true holiness. This is not a once-for-all transaction but a continual discipline, a lifelong battle against the remaining corruption in our nature. John Owen, in his masterful work 'The Mortification of Sin,' taught that the old nature does not die quietly. It fights, it schemes, it disguises itself, and the moment we relax our vigilance, it surges back with terrible force. But we do not fight alone. The Spirit who regenerated us is the Spirit who sanctifies us, and His power is infinitely greater than the power of sin. Thomas Brooks, in his 'Precious Remedies Against Satan's Devices,' catalogued dozens of strategies the enemy uses to draw believers back into the patterns of the old life. But against every device of Satan, there is a remedy in Christ. Against every temptation, there is a promise. Against every weakness, there is grace sufficient. The key is to be renewed in the spirit of our mind -- to think differently, to see the world through the lens of Scripture, to evaluate everything by the standard of Christ. As a man thinks in his heart, so is he. When our minds are renewed, our lives follow. Amen.
