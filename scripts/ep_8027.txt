PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"The Shield of Faith"
July 27, 2026

---

"Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God." -- Ephesians 6:15-17

'Above all, taking the shield of faith.' The Roman shield was large enough to cover the whole body, and it was soaked in water before battle to extinguish the flaming arrows of the enemy. So faith covers the whole man and quenches every fiery dart of doubt, fear, temptation, and accusation that Satan hurls against us. The Puritan John Bunyan portrayed this beautifully in 'The Pilgrim's Progress' when Christian passed through the Valley of the Shadow of Death. Every kind of horror assailed him, but he pressed on with his sword drawn and his shield raised, crying out, 'I will walk in the strength of the Lord God.' The helmet of salvation protects the mind -- the assurance of our salvation guards our thoughts from despair and keeps us fighting when everything within us wants to surrender. And the sword of the Spirit, the Word of God, is our only offensive weapon. Every other piece of armor is defensive; this alone enables us to attack. Jesus Himself demonstrated its use in the wilderness when He answered every temptation of Satan with 'It is written.' The believer who knows his Bible, who has hidden God's Word in his heart, who can wield the Scriptures with precision and power, is a terror to the forces of darkness. Amen.
