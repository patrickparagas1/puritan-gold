PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 17
"God Fights for Your Marriage"
Romans for Marriage — Day 17

---

Welcome, dear ones. Marriage is a gift from God, and so is this time together in His presence. Whether this has been an easy day or a hard one, you are here, together, and that matters more than you know.

Our Scripture reading this evening is from Romans 8:31-32. Let us receive it together:

"What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us? He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?"

That is Romans 8:31-32. Take a breath. Let those words settle between the two of you. God is speaking to your marriage through this passage.

--- Puritan Commentary ---

The Puritans understood marriage in a way that the modern world has largely forgotten. Let us hear from Thomas Goodwin, who wrote with great wisdom on this subject.

Paul asks one of the most powerful rhetorical questions in all of literature: 'If God be for us, who can be against us?' The implied answer is thunderous: no one. No adversary, no obstacle, no trial, no enemy — visible or invisible — can successfully oppose those for whom God fights. And the proof of God's commitment to fight for us is the cross: 'He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all.'

Thomas Goodwin spent years meditating on the depth of divine love revealed at the cross. He wrote that if God gave His greatest treasure — His only Son — for our salvation, then there is nothing lesser that He would withhold. The cross is the ultimate guarantee. If God paid the highest price imaginable to redeem you, He will certainly provide whatever else you need. It would be absurd to think that a God who gave His Son would withhold anything good.

For marriage, this means God is fighting for you. He is not a distant observer watching your marriage struggle. He is an active warrior who has already demonstrated His commitment by sacrificing His Son. If He went that far, He will not abandon your marriage now. Whatever you face — financial pressure, communication breakdown, spiritual warfare, relational wounds — you face it with the God of the universe on your side.

Goodwin observed that many believers live as though God were neutral or even opposed to their happiness. They pray timidly, as if trying to convince a reluctant deity to help. But Romans 8:31-32 demolishes that picture. God is for you. Aggressively, actively, sacrificially for you. He has proven it with blood. He will prove it again with every provision, every protection, and every act of grace you need.

'How shall he not with him also freely give us all things?' The logic is irresistible. If God gave you Christ — the greatest gift — will He withhold the lesser gifts? Will He refuse wisdom when you need it? Will He deny strength when you are weak? Will He withdraw grace when you stumble? Impossible. He gave His Son. Everything else is small by comparison.

--- Marriage Application ---

Your marriage has an advocate — and His name is God. He is not watching your marriage from a distance, hoping it works out. He is actively fighting for your covenant, providing what you need, and protecting what He has joined together.

When you face opposition — whether from external circumstances or internal struggles — remind each other: 'If God be for us, who can be against us?' This is not naive optimism. This is theological certainty backed by the blood of the cross.

Tonight, identify the biggest challenge your marriage is currently facing. Then read Romans 8:31-32 aloud over that challenge. Declare together: God is for our marriage. He gave His Son for us. He will freely give us everything we need to overcome this.

The Puritans understood that marriage is not a destination but a journey. Every day presents new opportunities to love, to serve, to forgive, and to grow together. The truths we have studied tonight are not just for tonight. They are for tomorrow morning when the alarm goes off, for the difficult conversation that has been waiting, for the quiet moments when only God sees how you treat each other.

--- Reflection ---

The Puritans practiced what they called holy conference, speaking honestly about spiritual things with those they loved most. Do that now with these questions.

Question 1: Do you live as though God is for your marriage or against it? How does your daily attitude reflect your belief?

Question 2: What is the biggest challenge your marriage faces right now? How does knowing God fights for you change your perspective?

Question 3: If God gave His Son for you, what lesser thing could He possibly withhold from your marriage?

Take a few minutes with each question. Do not rush. The best conversations in marriage happen when both people feel truly heard.

--- Prayer ---

It is time to bring what we have heard and discussed before the Lord in prayer. Pray out loud if you can. Let your spouse hear your heart.

Tonight's prayer focus: Declare together: God is for us. Pray boldly and confidently, knowing that the One who gave His Son will freely give you everything your marriage needs. Fight from victory, not toward it — the battle is already won.

If one of you finds it difficult to pray out loud, that is perfectly fine. Simply holding hands in silence before the Lord is itself a powerful act of worship. Let the Spirit intercede where words fail.

--- Blessing ---

May the God who spared not His own Son fight for your marriage with all-conquering love. If He is for you, nothing can stand against you. Walk in that confidence today and every day.

May the God who brought you together continue to bind you together in love, in faith, and in purpose. Sleep well tonight. He watches over your marriage.
