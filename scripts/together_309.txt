PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 9
"Choosing Wisdom's Feast"
Proverbs 9 -- Day 9 -- Couples Devotional

---

Hello, beloved couple. It is a beautiful thing when husband and wife sit down together in God's Word. Welcome to your couples devotional time.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs chapter 9. Let me read our key passage for this evening:

"The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding."
That is Proverbs 9:10.

Proverbs nine sets before us two invitations: Wisdom's feast and Folly's feast. Every day in your marriage, you choose which table to sit at. The conversations you have, the way you resolve conflict, the priorities you set, these all determine which feast you are attending.

Thomas Watson wrote: Thomas Watson wrote that every day sets before us two feasts: the feast of Wisdom, which nourishes the soul unto eternal life, and the feast of Folly, which poisons the heart unto destruction. Choose wisely, for your very life depends upon it.

Choose Wisdom's feast together. Fill your marriage with the nourishment of God's Word, with prayer, with honest conversation, and with the sweetness of mutual encouragement. The feast of Folly may look attractive for a moment, but its end is bitterness.

--- Reflection ---

Here is a question for you and your spouse to discuss together:

What choices are we making daily as a couple? Are we accepting Wisdom's invitation or drifting toward foolish patterns?

Take a few minutes to share honestly with each other. Listen without interrupting. Let this be a safe place for both of you to speak from the heart.

--- Prayer ---

Pray for discernment in your daily choices. Ask God to help you both recognize Wisdom's voice and choose her feast every day.

If you are able, hold hands as you pray together. There is something powerful about a husband and wife joining hands before the throne of grace.

Sleep well tonight, knowing that God is watching over your marriage with the same faithfulness He has shown to His people for generations.
