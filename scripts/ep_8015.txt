PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Exceeding Abundantly Above All We Ask"
July 15, 2026

---

"Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us, unto him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus throughout all ages, world without end. Amen." -- Ephesians 3:20-21

Paul concludes the doctrinal portion of Ephesians with this magnificent doxology. God is able to do what we ask. He is able to do above what we ask. He is able to do abundantly above what we ask. He is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think. Each word piles upon the last, as if Paul cannot find language adequate to express the limitless capacity of God's power and generosity. As the Puritan Thomas Manton wrote, 'Prayer is the measure of our desires, but God's giving exceeds even the measure of our prayers.' We ask for a drop, and He gives an ocean. We ask for a crumb, and He spreads a feast. We ask for strength to endure, and He gives power to triumph. The key to unlocking this power is found in those words 'according to the power that worketh in us.' The same Holy Spirit who raised Christ from the dead is the power at work within every believer. We are not drawing upon an external resource; we carry within us the very power of God. And this power is not a static deposit but a living, active, working force that transforms us from glory to glory. May we never limit God by the smallness of our prayers. May we dare to ask great things, expect great things, and give Him glory when He does exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think. Amen.
