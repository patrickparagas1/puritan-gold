PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Gifts for the Building Up of the Body"
July 17, 2026

---

"But unto every one of us is given grace according to the measure of the gift of Christ. And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers; for the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ." -- Ephesians 4:7-16

Christ ascended to heaven not to leave His church helpless, but to equip her with everything she needs. He gave gifts -- not primarily miraculous gifts for individual display, but ministry gifts for corporate edification. Apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers -- these are Christ's gift to His church, given for the perfecting of the saints and the building up of the body. Richard Baxter, in his 'Reformed Pastor,' wrote with passionate urgency about the responsibility of those called to shepherd God's flock. The purpose of all ministry, Paul says, is to bring every believer to maturity -- to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ. We are not to remain spiritual infants, tossed about by every wind of doctrine. We are to grow up in all things into Christ, who is the head. And this growth happens not in isolation but in community -- each joint supplying, each part working, each member contributing to the health and growth of the whole body. The individualism of our age is foreign to the New Testament. God has designed us to need one another, and the body of Christ functions properly only when every member fulfills its God-given role. Amen.
