PURITAN GOLD -- Family Devotional, Day 11
"Honesty and Kindness"
Proverbs 11 -- Proverbs 11 -- Family Devotional

---

Good evening, dear family. Welcome to tonight's family devotional from Puritan Gold.

Tonight we are in Proverbs chapter 11. Let me read our key passage:

"A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight. When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom. The integrity of the upright shall guide them."
That is Proverbs 11:1-3.

Proverbs chapter 11 begins with something that might seem unusual in a book of wisdom: a lesson about honest weights and measures. A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight. In the ancient marketplace, dishonest merchants would use rigged scales to cheat their customers. And God says, I hate that.

Why? Because God is a God of truth. He cannot lie, and He cannot stand lying. And this goes far beyond marketplace scales. It applies to everything. Are we honest in our words? Are we fair in how we treat others? Do we tell the truth even when it costs us something?

Thomas Brooks wrote that integrity means being the same person in private that you are in public. It means that if God pulled back the curtain and showed the whole world what you do when nobody is watching, you would not be ashamed.

And here is the beautiful thing: when we walk in honesty and integrity, we experience the delight of the Lord. He does not just tolerate honesty. He delights in it. Let us be a family that walks in truth, in big things and small.

Now, here is our memory verse for today. Let us say it together:

"A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight. -- Proverbs 11:1"

Try to say it from memory before we go to bed tonight. And tomorrow, see if you can remember it still!

Here are some questions for our family to discuss:

Number 1: What does it mean to be honest in big and small things?

Number 2: Why does God care about fairness?

Number 3: How can our family practice generosity this week?

Let us pray together. Lord God, we thank You for this time as a family. Thank You for speaking to us through Proverbs chapter 11. Plant these truths deep in our hearts, especially in the hearts of our children. Help us to walk in wisdom tomorrow and every day. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Goodnight, dear ones. Walk in wisdom tomorrow.
