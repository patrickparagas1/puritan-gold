PURITAN GOLD -- Together: Couples Devotional, Day 30
"Trusting Every Word of God"
Proverbs 30:5

---

Hello, beloved couple. It is a beautiful thing when husband and wife sit down together in God's Word. The Puritans called marriage a little church within the church. Tonight, your little church is in session. Welcome.

Tonight we turn to Proverbs 30:5. Listen with your hearts, not just your ears:

"Every word of God is pure: he is a shield unto them that put their trust in him."

That is Proverbs 30:5. Take a breath. Let those words settle between the two of you. God is speaking to your marriage through this passage.

--- Puritan Commentary ---

Matthew Henry had deep insight into the truths we have just read. The Puritans did not separate theology from daily life, and their reflections on marriage are remarkably practical.

Agur, the author of Proverbs 30, makes a declaration that is the foundation of all Christian faith and life: every word of God is pure. The Hebrew word (tsaraph) means 'refined, tested, proven' — like metal purified in a furnace. Matthew Henry writes: 'Every word of God is tried and found faithful; there is nothing in it that is dross or can deceive.'

Henry expands this claim to its full scope: every word. Not merely the promises we find comforting, but the commands we find demanding. Not merely the passages that affirm our preferences, but the passages that challenge our assumptions. Not merely the words that speak of God's love, but the words that speak of His holiness, His justice, and His wrath. Every word has been tested in the furnace of divine perfection and found to be without impurity.

The practical result is magnificent: 'He is a shield unto them that put their trust in him.' Henry connects the purity of God's Word to the protection of God's people. Because every word is reliable, those who trust in it are shielded. Henry writes: 'We cannot trust a man whose word is not sure. But God's word is perfectly pure, and therefore those that flee to him for refuge are perfectly safe.'

Charles Bridges applies this to the marriage: 'The couple who builds their life upon the pure Word of God has a shield that no adversity can penetrate. When trials come — and they will — the couple can turn to the Word and find it exactly as God promised: pure, reliable, protective.' Bridges warns that the couple who trusts selectively in God's Word — accepting the promises but ignoring the commands — will find their shield full of holes.

--- Marriage Application ---

Your marriage needs a standard that is higher than your feelings, your opinions, or your culture. That standard is the pure Word of God. Every word of it is tested, proven, and trustworthy.

When you face a moral dilemma, do you turn to God's Word? When you disagree about parenting, finances, or priorities, do you appeal to Scripture? When culture tells you that marriage means one thing and the Bible says another, which do you trust?

The couple who trusts every word of God finds Him to be a shield. This does not mean they will not face hardship, but that they will be protected in the midst of it. God's Word will guide your decisions, guard your hearts, and ground your marriage in a reality that cannot be shaken.

The Puritans understood that marriage is not a destination but a journey. Every day presents new opportunities to love, to serve, to forgive, and to grow together. The truths we have studied tonight are not just for tonight. They are for tomorrow morning when the alarm goes off, for the difficult conversation that has been waiting, for the quiet moments when only God sees how you treat each other.

--- Reflection ---

It is time for reflection. Turn to each other and consider these questions together. There is no rush. Let the conversation go wherever the Spirit leads.

Question 1: Are there any areas of our life where we are trusting our own judgment or cultural norms over God's pure Word?

Question 2: How has God's Word served as a shield for our marriage in the past?

Question 3: What is one passage of Scripture that we can commit to trusting together more deeply this month?

Take a few minutes with each question. Do not rush. The best conversations in marriage happen when both people feel truly heard.

--- Prayer ---

Now let us pray together. The Puritans said that a couple who prays together is bound by cords that cannot be broken. Let us strengthen those cords tonight.

Tonight's prayer focus: Affirm together your trust in every word of God. Ask Him to be your shield as a couple, and pray for the faith to rest upon His Word when circumstances are uncertain and the world's counsel contradicts His truth.

If one of you finds it difficult to pray out loud, that is perfectly fine. Simply holding hands in silence before the Lord is itself a powerful act of worship. Let the Spirit intercede where words fail.

--- Blessing ---

May the God whose every word is pure be your shield and your refuge, guarding your marriage with the impenetrable protection of His faithful promises. Amen.

The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you both, tonight and always. Goodnight.
