Precious Remedies Against Satan's Devices. By Thomas Brooks. A Complete Summary.

Welcome to this summary of Thomas Brooks's Precious Remedies Against Satan's Devices, one of the most practical and searching works on spiritual warfare in all of Christian literature. First published in 1652, this book exposes the cunning stratagems of the devil and provides powerful biblical remedies against each one.

Brooks demonstrates that Satan is the most subtle enemy the Christian faces. He does not always attack openly but uses devices — carefully crafted strategies designed to deceive, discourage, and destroy.

The first category of devices aims to draw the soul into sin. Satan presents the bait and hides the hook, making sin appear attractive while concealing its terrible consequences. He paints sin with virtue's colors, calling covetousness prudence and revenge justice. He extenuates sin, telling us it is but a small matter. He presents the sins of great saints like David and Peter to encourage us that we too may sin safely. And he presents God as all mercy, suggesting that a loving God would never punish.

Brooks provides multiple remedies for each device. Against the baited hook, he counsels us to keep at the greatest distance from sin and to consider that sin is a bitter sweet. Against painted sin, he urges us to look on sin with the eye of eternity. Against extenuated sin, he reminds us that the least sin contains infinite evil because it is committed against an infinitely holy God.

The second category of devices keeps the soul from holy duties — prayer, Scripture reading, and attendance upon the means of grace. Satan makes these duties seem burdensome, unprofitable, or unnecessary.

The third category aims to promote discord among the saints, dividing the body of Christ through suspicion, pride, and party spirit.

The fourth category seeks to fill the saints with despondency and despair by magnifying their sins, hiding God's mercy, and presenting sad thoughts of God's character.

Throughout, Brooks writes with warmth, wit, and deep pastoral concern. His remedies are firmly rooted in Scripture and seasoned with striking illustrations. This book remains essential reading for every Christian who desires to stand firm against the wiles of the devil.
