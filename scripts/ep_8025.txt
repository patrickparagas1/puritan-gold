PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"The Whole Armour of God"
July 25, 2026

---

"Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places." -- Ephesians 6:10-13

Paul's final exhortation is a call to arms. The Christian life is not a leisurely stroll through a garden; it is a fierce battle against unseen enemies of terrifying power. William Gurnall's magisterial work 'The Christian in Complete Armour' runs to over a thousand pages of exposition on this single passage, and he confessed that even after years of study, he had barely scratched the surface. Our enemies are not human; they are spiritual forces of wickedness in the heavenly realms. We cannot see them, but they can see us. We cannot match them in our own strength, but we need not try, for the armor we wear is God's armor, forged in the furnace of divine omnipotence. 'Be strong in the Lord' -- not in yourself, not in your gifts, not in your experience, but in the Lord and in the power of His might. As Thomas Brooks taught, the soldier who goes into battle trusting in his own sword will be cut down in the first engagement. But the soldier who goes in trusting the Captain of his salvation will stand when the smoke clears. Put on the whole armor -- not part of it, not most of it, but every piece. For the enemy is cunning, and he will attack wherever he finds us unprotected. Amen.
