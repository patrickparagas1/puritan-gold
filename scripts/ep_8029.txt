PURITAN GOLD -- Daily Study
"Grace Be with All Them That Love Our Lord"
July 29, 2026

---

"But that ye also may know my affairs, and how I do, Tychicus, a beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, shall make known to you all things. Peace be to the brethren, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. Grace be with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity. Amen." -- Ephesians 6:21-24

Paul closes this sublime epistle with a blessing of peace, love, faith, and grace. Four words that summarize the entire Christian life. Peace with God through the blood of the cross. Love poured into our hearts by the Holy Spirit. Faith as the instrument by which we receive every blessing. And grace -- always grace, first and last and everything in between. Notice the final condition: 'Grace be with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity.' The word 'sincerity' literally means 'incorruptibility.' Grace is for those who love Christ with an undying, imperishable, incorruptible love -- a love that endures through trial and temptation, through suffering and sorrow, through persecution and death. As Samuel Rutherford wrote from his prison cell, 'I have tasted Christ's love, and I am cured forever of any desire for what the world can offer.' May this be our testimony as we conclude our study of Ephesians. We have climbed the Alps of divine revelation. We have gazed upon the riches of grace, the mystery of the church, the calling to holiness, and the armor of God. Now let us walk worthy of all that we have received, and may grace -- sovereign, free, eternal, inexhaustible grace -- be with us all. Amen.
