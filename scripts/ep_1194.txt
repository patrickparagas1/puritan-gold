

PURITAN GOLD -- All of Grace: Complete Summary
A Comprehensive Overview of Spurgeon's Classic Work on Salvation by Grace


All of Grace: An Earnest Word with Those Who Are Seeking Salvation by the Lord Jesus Christ. By Charles Haddon Spurgeon. A Complete Summary.

This is a summary of Charles Spurgeon's beloved book All of Grace, published in 1894. It is one of the clearest and most warm-hearted presentations of the gospel ever penned. Spurgeon wrote it for the common person — for the poor, the uneducated, the struggling sinner — and its simplicity is its power.

THE AUTHOR AND HIS PURPOSE.

Charles Haddon Spurgeon was the most famous preacher of the Victorian era, pastor of the Metropolitan Tabernacle in London for thirty-eight years. He wrote All of Grace to lead souls to Christ. His stated aim was nothing less than the conversion of every reader.

The book contains 14 chapters, each short and direct, building a complete picture of how sinners are saved by the grace of God through faith in Jesus Christ.

Spurgeon begins by addressing the reader directly, urging them to give serious attention to the matter of their soul's salvation. He then demonstrates that God justifies the ungodly — not the righteous, but sinners — and that this justification comes entirely by grace through faith.

He explains what faith is, how it operates, and why God chose faith as the instrument of salvation. He addresses the cry of those who feel they can do nothing to save themselves, and assures them that this is precisely the condition God meets with His grace.

The book covers regeneration by the Holy Spirit, the necessity of repentance, the fear of falling away, and the assurance that true believers will persevere to the end. Spurgeon closes with a direct appeal to every reader to come to Christ without delay.

LASTING SIGNIFICANCE.

All of Grace has led countless souls to Christ since its publication. Its power lies in Spurgeon's gift for making the deepest truths of the gospel accessible to the simplest reader. It remains the ideal book to place in the hands of anyone seeking salvation.
